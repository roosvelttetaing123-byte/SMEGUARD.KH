# SMEGUARD.KH

**Know your next business task. Understand why. Keep the proof.**

A proposed Khmer-first operating assistant for Cambodian small businesses and their accountants. Eight short intake steps produce an initial roadmap; reviewed, versioned rules turn confirmed business facts into tasks, reminders and evidence requirements.

## Status — 10 September 2026

**Product discovery / docs-first foundation. Not a production compliance service.**

This repository is being initialized with a business and product blueprint, implementation guardrails, and a browser-only UX concept. No government integration, regulatory certification, customers, revenue, production backend, working document vault, or notification delivery is claimed.

## Product boundary

Free public guidance and initial planning; paid coordination, recurring reminders, private document management and accountant collaboration. Complement official Cambodian services and accounting software rather than recreate them. Eight steps are intake, not a guarantee that all legal facts are known.

**Rules determine eligibility and dates. AI may help explain approved evidence; it must never invent requirements or publish legal rules.**

## Proposed first delivery

A narrow, reviewed obligation pack for selected locally owned, single-site retail/service businesses; an owner workflow; an explicitly authorized accountant view; an auditable source/change process. Tax filing, tax calculation, payroll, autonomous submissions, unrestricted chat, bank integrations and government dashboards are outside the initial scope.

Read the blueprint's assumptions, source limitations and release gates before building. Commercial figures are hypotheses, not offers or forecasts. Do not add confidential business documents, customer information, credentials or government-only material to this public repository.
