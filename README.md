# SMEGUARD.KH

**Know your next business task. Understand why. Keep the proof.**

A proposed Khmer-first operating assistant for Cambodian small businesses and their accountants. Eight short intake steps produce an initial roadmap; reviewed, versioned rules would turn confirmed business facts into tasks, reminders and evidence requirements.

## Status — 10 September 2026

**Product discovery / docs-first foundation. Not a production compliance service.**

The repository contains a business/product blueprint, a primary-source competitive update, implementation guardrails, a browser-only UX concept and a record of its local smoke checks. No government integration, regulatory certification, customers, revenue, production backend, working document vault, payment service or notification delivery is claimed.

## Product boundary

Free public guidance and initial planning; paid cross-agency coordination, recurring reminders, private evidence management and accountant collaboration. Complement official Cambodian services and accounting software rather than recreate them. Eight steps are intake, not a guarantee that all legal facts are known.

GDT already offers tax-information AI, and BanhJi already targets MSMEs and their accountants. A generic Khmer tax chatbot or accountant dashboard is therefore not sufficient differentiation. The proposed gap is a recurring cross-agency obligation/change/evidence workflow; it must be demonstrated with customers, not assumed from competitor marketing pages.

**Rules determine eligibility and dates. AI may help explain approved evidence; it must never invent requirements or publish legal rules.**

## Proposed first delivery

A narrow, reviewed obligation pack for selected locally owned, single-site retail/service businesses; an owner workflow; an explicitly authorized accountant view; an auditable source/change process. Tax filing, tax calculation, payroll, autonomous submissions, unrestricted chat, bank integrations and government dashboards are outside the initial scope.

Read the competitive update and the blueprint's assumptions, source limitations and release gates before building. Commercial figures are hypotheses, not offers or forecasts.
