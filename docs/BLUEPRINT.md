# SMEGUARD.KH
## Business, product and delivery blueprint

**Version 0.1 | Research checked 10 September 2026 | Discovery-stage proposal**

**Know your next business task. Understand why. Keep the proof.**

This is a product/business plan, not a legal opinion or an approved Cambodian compliance rule pack. Prices, costs, conversion rates, capacity and impact figures below are explicitly proposed assumptions or targets. No pilots, partnerships, customers, approvals or revenue have been secured by this document.

## 1. Decision and positioning

Build SMEGUARD as a **small-business obligation and evidence workspace**, not as a Cambodian ERP and not as a general legal chatbot. The initial promise is: “Tell us about your business; see what needs attention next, why it may apply, who will handle it, and what proof to keep.”

The earlier CDA concept proposed eight questions, registration requirements, tax dates, employee obligations, license reminders, a document vault and expiry alerts, with every answer traced to a source. Preserve that scope and its simplicity principle. This blueprint adds a narrower paying customer, a content-governance model and explicit delivery gates; it does not treat the earlier optimism about commercial potential as market validation.

TRUST.KH and SMEGUARD have different strengths. TRUST is a natural prevention/public-value demonstration. SMEGUARD has a more direct proposed payer: an owner or accounting practice coordinating recurring obligations. That makes its revenue hypothesis easier to test, not proven. A source library with reminders alone is readily substitutable. The defensible product is the combination of reviewed rules, clear applicability, workflow history, permissions, accountant distribution and evidence that a task was handled.

Use “TurboTax for running a Cambodian SME” only as an internal analogy. Publicly it implies tax preparation, filing and assurance that the first version will not provide. Prefer **“Your business obligations, organized.”** Do not imply government ownership, endorsement, authorization, or domain registration merely because the product name ends in .KH.

### Commercial ownership versus ministry value

Treat these as separate decisions. A private startup can sell software while a ministry gains public-service benefits. A ministry-owned service would need its own ownership, procurement, charging and governance decisions; SaaS revenue cannot simply be described as ministry profit. Before commercialization, document intellectual-property ownership, employment permissions, conflicts of interest, approval to use institutional resources and any authorized branding. This is a governance checkpoint, not a claim that a particular legal structure has been approved.

Do not merge TRUST and SMEGUARD databases or customer profiles. Shared UI/authentication components may eventually be reusable, but the products have different data purposes and risk models. Keep the current TRUST competition work intact rather than rebuilding two complete products at once.

## 2. What the external evidence supports

### Existing infrastructure is a reason to complement, not duplicate

Cambodia already has the Online Business Registration service, including routes for registration and specialized licensing. Its FAQ says requirements and fees depend on enterprise form, requested services and other business facts. SMEGUARD should prepare and coordinate work, then direct the user to the appropriate official service. No API partnership is assumed. [R1]

KHMERSME already publishes registration, licensing and tax guidance. Its tax section explicitly identifies compliance costs and administration among SME concerns. This supports the existence of the information/coordination problem, but does not prove willingness to pay for another tool. [R2, R3]

BanhJi is a serious adjacent product, not an afterthought. Its current pricing page lists Free, Plus at KHR 60,000/month, Standard at KHR 120,000/month and Pro at KHR 180,000/month; its materials include accounting and tax compliance. These are observed advertised prices, not a quotation to this project. Avoid competing on bookkeeping, invoicing, payroll or financial reporting in the MVP. Test whether cross-agency obligations and owner–accountant coordination solve a separate job. [R4]

### Market context, not an inflated addressable market

The 2022 Economic Census was reported as counting **753,670 establishments**. That is historical establishment data, not a 2026 count of unique companies, registered taxpayers or subscription-ready customers. Do not multiply it by a monthly price and call the result a validated market. The NIS census index is the primary discovery route; the figure here is supported by contemporary reporting of the results. [R5, R6]

Use a bottom-up acquisition model instead: named accounting firms, the eligible client portfolios they actually manage, specific owner interviews, and measured conversion. Count unique business IDs, not branch locations, twice. Count paying accounting practices separately from the client businesses they cover.

### Source links alone do not establish correctness

A concrete research warning: NSSF's English registration page still references a 2008 registration period, while a later professional explanation discusses Prakas 168 dated 5 July 2022. These are not interchangeable. This research did not complete an operative-Khmer-instrument and amendment review for a publishable NSSF rule pack. Treat exact NSSF deadlines, thresholds and contribution calculations as **blocked for publication pending qualified review**. A government domain or a freshly crawled page is not proof that every statement is currently operative. [R7, R8]

Similarly, GDT's indexed current calendar distinguishes August 2026 manual filing on **21 September 2026** from online filing on **25 September 2026**. This is a useful example of why filing channel and period matter, not a universal monthly rule or a finding that every business must file. The calendar was visible in search retrieval; a direct fetch of that particular article failed. Capture the authoritative notice/calendar evidence and verify applicability before converting it into a production deadline. [R9]

Potential tax incentives require their own eligibility and expiry checks. Do not make a blanket “all new SMEs are tax-exempt” statement or import benefits from a news summary. Incentive eligibility should initially become a reviewer question, not an automated entitlement. [R3]

## 3. First customers and product boundary

### First paying-customer hypothesis

Target Cambodian-owned, single-site, already operating ordinary retail and service businesses with roughly 3–20 staff, plus the small accounting practices that serve them. This headcount range is a marketing hypothesis, **not a legal classification or threshold**. Start with a carefully selected local pilot, not every sector nationwide.

Prefer owners who already spend time sending certificates, asking about deadlines and chasing an accountant for task status. A practice managing 10–30 eligible businesses is potentially a stronger buyer than a one-person informal stall: one account purchase can support several client workflows. Validate this directly; an accountant may already have an adequate spreadsheet or an existing software feature.

Unregistered microbusinesses can use the free preparation guide, but should not be the assumed first subscription engine. Restaurants, clinics, pharmaceuticals, tourism, finance, schools, manufacturing, import/export, foreign staff, foreign ownership and multi-site structures require extra coverage or specialist review. Detect them without pretending that the pilot rules cover them.

### The recurring job customers pay for

**Before the deadline:** identify the next action, assign it, request missing documents, and follow up.

**After the action:** attach a receipt or confirmation, record who reviewed it, preserve the history and generate the next occurrence where applicable.

**When something changes:** explain which previously reviewed instruction changed, which businesses may be affected, and what the owner/accountant must confirm.

Free information remains accessible. Charge for durable coordination, private workspace features and reliable delivery—not access to an official regulation.

### In the MVP

Eight-step intake; a small reviewed rule catalog; one owner task list; source/applicability cards; a simple dated view; owner-confirmed document metadata and a protected vault after security gates; opt-in reminders; revocable accountant access; a PDF business action plan; a minimal rule-review console; event history and a feedback channel.

### Not in the MVP

Tax calculation or filing, payroll, full accounting, invoicing, point of sale, automatic government submissions, bank account connections, native mobile apps, general-purpose chat, credit scoring, national enforcement dashboards, every business sector, custom model training, blockchain, Kubernetes and a microservice fleet.

## 4. Simplicity: eight steps, then the next useful action

Eight answers produce an **initial roadmap**, not a legal certificate. Missing facts must remain visible. Ask targeted follow-ups only when needed, such as filing channel, tax period, registration date, ownership, fiscal year, rent arrangements, employee start date, or the exact activity of a branch.

| Step | Plain-language question | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What does your business do? | Select a supported activity or route to review. |
| 2 | Where does it operate? | Establish geographic coverage; do not guess local permits. |
| 3 | Are you planning to open or already operating? | Separate preparation from recurring work. |
| 4 | What is its legal form? | Record sole proprietor/company/unknown without guessing. |
| 5 | Which registrations are already completed? | Distinguish preparation from evidence collection. |
| 6 | Do you know its official taxpayer classification? | Record confirmed status or a question for the accountant. |
| 7 | Does it have employees? | Trigger workforce applicability review, not payroll calculations. |
| 8 | Are there special circumstances? | Flag online sales, imports, foreign staff, multiple locations and other complex cases. |

Allow “I do not know” everywhere it is meaningful. No national ID, bank information, employee salary, or full tax document is necessary to demonstrate initial value. Guest planning should not require registration; saving private information requires an authenticated account and explicit notices.

### Four owner destinations

**Today | Calendar | Documents | My business**

Show at most three priority actions above the fold. The primary action changes with the task: “Confirm business details”, “Send to accountant”, “Add proof” or “Open official service”. Avoid a homepage filled with charts, laws and percentages.

A proposed task card:

> **Confirm your next monthly filing deadline**  
> Your tax period and filing channel still need confirmation.  
> Owner: Your accountant · Status: Needs information  
> Why this appears: You selected an already registered business.  
> Source: GDT calendar/guidance · Legal applicability: not yet approved  
> **Confirm details**

A business-selected preparation target is different from a statutory due date. A certificate expiry read from a file is different from a legally established renewal obligation. Label those date types separately throughout the UI, reminders and PDF.

### Visual direction

Use a calm service interface rather than cyber-security theatre: deep green/navy, warm off-white surfaces, restrained amber for attention, clear text labels and ample whitespace. Do not use an official seal. Design for a small phone first; use approximately 18px body text, generous Khmer line height, large touch targets and no clipping of Khmer marks. These are design targets to validate, not measured accessibility compliance.

Production should be Khmer-first with a human-reviewed glossary and English fallback. Keep source instrument titles and article references intact. The concept prototype is intentionally English-led and is not a completed Khmer localization. Have target users read action wording aloud; translation quality is a release gate, not a cosmetic final pass.

A working indicator should say what is actually happening: “Preparing your checklist” or “Uploading document”. Never animate “Verifying with government” unless a real authorized integration is running. Show actionable failure states, preserve entered information and allow retry without duplicate operations.

## 5. Regulatory accuracy: the real product engine

### Two different checks

**Applicability:** Does a reviewed rule apply to this business, in this location, for this period and filing method?

**Completion:** What evidence shows that this particular occurrence was handled?

Keep them separate. Checking a box, possessing a certificate or uploading a receipt does not itself establish legal compliance. Avoid “100% compliant”, green compliance scores, guarantees of no fines and official-looking clearance certificates. Use “No open tasks in your currently covered checklist” with visible coverage limits.

### Rule record

Each production rule needs at least: stable rule ID; jurisdiction and geographic scope; instrument and issuing authority; specific article/section or authoritative calendar entry; original source location; retained lawful evidence snapshot and hash; language and translation status; effective-from and effective-to dates; publication and retrieval dates; review date and reviewer role; supersession links; applicability predicate; required facts; exceptions; deadline calculation policy; holiday/extension handling; evidence requirements; and automated test cases.

Maintain both **legal effective time** and **system knowledge/review time**. An announcement discovered today may affect a previous period or a future one. Preserve the rule version that generated a task and PDF so the answer can be reconstructed later.

### Publication workflow

Candidate source → authenticity/authority check → extraction → qualified Cambodian subject-matter review → rule implementation → positive/negative/boundary tests → independent publication approval → versioned release → affected-business evaluation → correction notice where needed.

One engineer can draft the rule; a suitable local professional must review substantive tax/labor interpretation. Do not describe a reviewer as government-approved unless that is actually established. Reserve a human review budget and a backup reviewer. Have the founder approve scope and release gates, not pretend that AI is a licensed professional.

Prefer operative primary Khmer instruments and verified authority notices. Clearly identify translations and professional commentary as such. A secondary explainer is a discovery aid, not enough by itself to activate a legally consequential rule. Automated source watching should create review tickets, never publish revised obligations autonomously.

### Uncertainty states

A candidate can be applicable, not applicable, missing information, outside coverage, awaiting legal review, stale or superseded. Do not reduce every state to true/false. Unknown must not become “not required”. A stale rule remains in historical evidence but must not silently generate new binding instructions.

When an approved source is temporarily unavailable, continue showing the stored reviewed version with its verification status and review policy. When a potentially material change is detected, mark affected guidance under review. Do not erase existing deadlines; distinguish the previous published date from a proposed revision until the review resolves it.

### Initial content inventory and gates

| Obligation family | Candidate authority/source | What is safe at discovery stage | Publication blocker |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business registration | Official registration service and relevant MoC instruments | Preparation and official-service navigation | Confirm entity form, exact document list and current instruments. |
| Monthly/annual tax tasks and patent tax | GDT notices, calendar and operative instruments | Identify required facts and candidate task families | Review taxpayer class, period, filing channel, exclusions, fees and extensions. |
| Employees and NSSF | MLVT/NSSF operative instruments | Workforce-review prompt | Complete current Khmer source and amendment review. |
| Local/sector permissions | Relevant authority and local service source | Flag unsupported activities/locations | Validate each sector/location instead of generic national rules. |
| Document expiry | User-confirmed document field | Personal reminder with source/provenance label | Confirm the date; do not infer a statutory renewal period. |
| Incentives or exemptions | Applicable GDT instruments and approval evidence | Ask reviewer about possible eligibility | Verify conditions, filing duties, evidence and expiry. |

The intended pilot catalog is approximately **15–25 reviewed rule templates**, not 25 rules already approved. Do not launch nationwide legal coverage based on this document.

## 6. Business model and unit economics

### Pricing hypotheses to test

| Package | Proposed monthly price | Included job |
|---|---:|---|
| Planner | Free | Initial roadmap, public source cards and simple downloadable plan; no private vault or delivery SLA. |
| Owner | USD 6 | One business; saved tasks, reminders, a bounded vault and one invited accountant. |
| Practice | USD 49 | Up to 10 client businesses; client work queue, document requests, assignments and audit history. |
| Additional practice client | USD 3 | One additional explicitly authorized business. |

Do not double-bill the same business as a direct Owner subscriber and an included Practice client. Define seat, storage, notification and export limits before selling; no unlimited human tax advice is included. Professional work, where offered later by an appropriately qualified provider, is separately priced and disclosed. Never sell regulatory priority or expedited government approval.

Use transparent invoices for an early paid pilot. Payment-provider availability, fees, business eligibility and recurring-payment support must be verified before integration. A KHQR payment screenshot is not proof of settlement. Do not describe manual payment reconciliation as an automated subscription engine. Government fees, advisor fees and software subscription fees must remain separate.

### Illustrative monthly economics

The following is a sensitivity model, not a forecast or supplier quotation. Assume each direct Owner costs **USD 1.50/month** in variable hosting/storage/messaging/support provision; each Practice account with 10 active clients costs **USD 12/month**. Assume a **3% collection fee** on collected subscription revenue. The 3% rate is a planning input, not a verified Cambodian gateway rate.

Owner contribution = 6 − 1.50 − 0.18 = **USD 4.32/month**. Practice contribution = 49 − 12 − 1.47 = **USD 35.53/month**. This is contribution margin before shared content review, fixed operating expense, founder pay, taxes and acquisition expense.

Illustrative fixed operating allowance: infrastructure/monitoring USD 150, professional content review USD 500, administration/materials USD 150, and ongoing channel work USD 200 = **USD 1,000/month**. Separately assume **USD 1,600/month combined founder compensation**. These are budgeting assumptions, not market salaries or contractor quotations; obtain real quotations and avoid counting the same support cost twice.

| Scenario | Direct Owners | Practices | Covered businesses* | Monthly recurring revenue | Contribution | After USD 1,000 fixed cost | After additional USD 1,600 founder pay |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pilot | 50 | 3 | 80 | 447.00 | 322.59 | -677.41 | -2,277.41 |
| Early traction | 300 | 20 | 500 | 2,780.00 | 2,006.60 | 1,006.60 | -593.40 |
| Growth illustration | 1,000 | 60 | 1,600 | 8,940.00 | 6,451.80 | 5,451.80 | 3,851.80 |

*Assumes 10 active included clients per practice, no overlap with direct subscriptions, no extra-client fees and full monthly collection. Actual practice activation may be much lower. Larger-scale fixed costs probably increase, so the last column is not a promised net profit. It excludes tax, refunds, bad debts, one-off launch costs and changes in acquisition spending.*

Under this simplified cost structure, about **602 direct Owners** or **74 full-price Practices** would cover USD 2,600/month of shared cost and founder compensation. A mixed example, **600 Owners + 20 Practices**, produces USD 4,580 MRR and approximately USD 702.60 remaining after the stated variable and fixed assumptions. This illustrates why “50 paying shops” is validation, not yet a sustainable business.

Treat an acquisition cost ceiling of USD 15/direct Owner and USD 100/Practice as a test hypothesis. At the assumed contribution, payback would be about 3.5 and 2.8 months respectively, before churn effects. Measure support minutes, successful collections, renewal and cancellation reasons; do not manufacture lifetime value from a few weeks of data.

A sustainable national or regional business also needs an operating reserve and liability/insurance review. Raising prices or constraining service is preferable to disguising unlimited professional advice inside a USD 6 product.

## 7. Go-to-market and validation

### Sell to the accountant; remain useful to the owner

The channel hypothesis is a practice that replaces repeated chasing across chat threads and spreadsheets with a shared task/evidence workflow. The owner receives clear next actions; the accountant gets a cross-client queue. Do not position the product as replacing accountants—the expert is part of its trust model.

First discovery target: **10 owners and 5 accounting practices**. Ask each to reconstruct a recent deadline/document episode, show the actual workflow with sensitive information removed, identify who performs the work, quantify minutes spent, identify the existing substitute and state who controls the budget. Ask for a paid trial at a concrete price, not just “Would this be useful?”

First pilot target: **3 practices and 15–30 consenting businesses** within the reviewed scope. These are recruitment targets, not established partners. Use a clear pilot agreement, support boundary, consent and deletion path. Start with low-sensitivity certificate metadata and synthetic training documents until vault controls pass.

A meaningful pilot measures recurring work, not registrations to a landing page. Compare a common task before/after using the same definition and record sample size and limitations. Timing a document request or preparing a checklist can be measured in weeks; reductions in annual fines or national formalization cannot be inferred from a short pilot.

### Decision gates

By the first two weeks of field testing, seek at least 3 genuine paid trial commitments or equivalent completed paid trials from independent customers; do not count complimentary accounts or friendly promises as revenue validation. By the end of the pilot, seek repeat use through at least one real recurring work cycle and a decision on renewal. Annual compliance retention remains untested.

Candidate product targets: 80% of test participants complete intake without help; median intake under 3 minutes; an assisted first useful action under 10 minutes; zero known critical wrong-obligation/wrong-date errors in the approved test set; complete source provenance for all published rules; and a measured decline in chasing/preparation time. Report the denominator, dates, conditions and unresolved issues. None is a claim about the current prototype.

Pause or narrow the product if owners value only a free checklist, practices will not pay, the existing accounting tool already solves the job, review costs consume the margin, or support exceeds the pricing model. Do not add more features to hide a weak paid-demand signal.

## 8. Architecture: one app, clear boundaries

### Proposed two-person stack

Use a **FastAPI modular monolith**, server-rendered HTML/Jinja plus focused JavaScript, PostgreSQL, migrations and S3-compatible private object storage. A PWA-style responsive experience is sufficient initially. Use a bounded background worker from the same codebase for reminders, PDF creation and document processing. Keep the runtime portable in containers. Validate supported dependency versions and pin them when implementation begins; this blueprint is not a tested dependency lockfile.

A large separate SPA, multiple backend languages and a graph/vector database are not required for the first obligation catalog. An approved-source search feature can be added when a concrete user job warrants it.

```text
Owner / invited accountant / rule reviewer
                    |
       Responsive web + authenticated API
                    |
          FastAPI modular application
          |        |         |        |
       Profiles  Rules     Tasks    Permissions
          |        |         |        |
                PostgreSQL
                 |       |
             Outbox    Audit history
                 |
          Bounded job worker
          |        |         |
       Reminders   PDFs   Document quarantine
                              |
                       Private object storage
```

This is proposed architecture. The browser concept included in the repository does not implement the backend, private storage, real notification delivery or source approval.

### Core domain entities

Organizations/businesses; user memberships; explicit advisor grants; versioned business profiles; authority/source records; source snapshots; rule versions; rule tests and review decisions; evaluation runs; obligation occurrences; document metadata and versions; task events; notification outbox/delivery attempts; and audit events.

Every private record must be organization-scoped. A practice does not automatically own its clients' data. Clients grant limited, revocable access; scopes distinguish viewing, editing tasks, uploading documents and inviting others. Revocation must also invalidate cached authorization and future background work, not just hide the UI.

### Deterministic evaluation

Take a confirmed profile revision, evaluation date and approved rule-pack version. Produce matched, unmatched, unknown and out-of-scope results with reasons. Each task records the rule, profile and evaluation versions, applicable period, due-date basis and any exception. Never treat a model's output as an executable rule or SQL expression.

Add unique constraints for obligation occurrences, such as organization + logical rule + obligation period, while storing revisions separately. Re-evaluation must not duplicate tasks or erase completion evidence. If a rule changes, preserve the previous record, create an auditable revision and notify the responsible person of a material change.

### Reliable reminders

Use a database outbox written in the same transaction as the task change. A worker claims jobs with leases, retries temporary failures with backoff, caps attempts and surfaces dead-letter failures. Deduplicate by task occurrence, channel, reminder slot and revision. Revalidate user access, task status, source status and consent immediately before sending.

Store UTC timestamps and derive Cambodian business deadlines using **Asia/Phnom_Penh**. Preserve the intended local date and applicable deadline policy. Do not hard-code “20th/25th every month” or move every holiday date automatically without an approved rule. A worker restart must neither silently lose reminders nor promise exactly-once delivery from a third-party provider.

Notifications contain a minimal task notice and a normal authenticated destination, not salary, tax documents or permanent bearer links. Provider acceptance is not the same as delivery, and delivery is not proof that the owner read the notice.

## 9. Security, privacy and operations

Use authentication, server-side authorization, least-privileged roles, secure cookies and CSRF controls where applicable, rate limits, input validation, encrypted transport and storage, audit trails, monitored backups, restore drills and a documented incident path. Review the actual Cambodian legal/data-governance position with qualified counsel before accepting real business records; this blueprint does not assert comprehensive regulatory compliance.

PostgreSQL row-level security can provide additional tenant isolation, but normal table owners and privileged roles can bypass protections. Use a non-owner application role, appropriate FORCE RLS/policies and explicit cross-tenant tests; RLS is not a substitute for API authorization. Test background workers, exports, search, document downloads and advisor revocation too. [R10]

A vault needs private buckets, checked object access, short-lived download capability, malware quarantine, file type/size/page limits, safe filenames, parser isolation and no sensitive content in logs. Avoid retaining national IDs and payroll documents in the first pilot unless genuinely necessary. Do not send raw private documents to external AI by default. Extracted expiry dates require owner/advisor confirmation before activating reminders.

Source documents and uploads are untrusted input. Treat embedded instructions as data. A source watcher must not fetch arbitrary user-supplied URLs from privileged infrastructure. An explanation model can paraphrase approved evidence and cite stored source IDs; it cannot invent a citation, select a statute from unrestricted web search, amend the rule catalog or send a notification autonomously.

Do not cache authenticated vault pages in a shared service worker or browser storage. Offer safe exports and a retention/deletion policy that distinguishes active data, legally retained records and backup expiry. Do not promise instantaneous deletion from all backups unless implemented and lawful.

Government/association reporting, if added later, should use consented, purpose-limited aggregation, suppress small identifying cohorts and avoid publishing business-by-business noncompliance lists. Private reminders must not become a surveillance feed or a lead list sold to advisers without consent.

### Hosting discipline

Render is a possible pilot host, not a scalability proof. Its documentation says free web services sleep after 15 idle minutes and local changes are ephemeral; free PostgreSQL expires after 30 days and has no backups. Use a paid always-on service, durable managed database, independent job execution and private object storage before relying on reminders or collecting real vault documents. No deployment or paid resource is created by this blueprint. [R11]

## 10. Scalability: engineering, content and distribution

### Technical scaling

Start with one web application, one durable database and a worker. Move to multiple stateless web instances and additional workers after measured load justifies it. Keep files in object storage rather than local disks; cache approved public content by rule-pack version; index tenant/date/status queries; paginate practice queues; monitor database connections, queue age and failed notifications. Add Redis or another queue only when the database-outbox approach demonstrably needs it.

Illustrative sizing: 10,000 businesses × 20 active tasks = 200,000 task records; 10,000 × 12 reminders/month = 120,000 reminders/month, roughly 4,000/day over 30 days. The challenge is deadline clustering, retries and provider throttling, not the average. At 20 documents × 2 MB/business, 10,000 businesses imply roughly 400 GB before versions, replicas and backups. These are workload assumptions, not tested capacity.

A load-test plan should separately measure profile evaluation, owner dashboard, practice queues, uploads, bulk rule-change fanout and reminder bursts. Suggested pilot acceptance targets are p95 dashboard response under 2 seconds at 50 concurrent test users and recoverable queued work after restart. Record hardware, database size, workload and error rate. Do not claim “supports a million SMEs” without those measurements and an operating model.

### Content scaling

A reviewed rule is shared, while its applicability is evaluated against each business. A new legal version should trigger evaluation of the affected cohort, not a full LLM research session for every user. Add sectors and provinces as separately reviewed coverage packs. Track outstanding source reviews, stale published rules, correction time and affected-business counts. Reviewer capacity, source changes and support costs must scale with coverage.

### Distribution scaling

One accounting practice can onboard multiple client businesses, subject to individual authority and consent. Associations or business-support programs may later sponsor access, but no partnership is assumed. Government can be an optional customer for a properly governed deployment, not the sole route to revenue.

Regional expansion reuses workflow, permissions and audit machinery. Laws, languages, professional review, holidays, forms and licensing remain country-specific work. A new language pack alone is not a new-country launch.

### Judge-ready explanation

> “We separate the software workflow from the reviewed country rules. A rule update is reviewed once, then evaluated against the businesses it affects. Web servers and background workers can grow separately, while private documents remain in durable storage. We prove the next scale with load tests and reviewer capacity—not just a claim that the cloud is scalable.”

## 11. Delivery plan and release gates

The official CDA 2026 page lists **2 November 2026** as the application deadline, **19 November** for semifinal judging, **1 December** for final judging and **11 December** for the ceremony. From 10 September to 2 November there are 53 days. The Minister's Startup category advertises USD 15,000 and a startup age limit of three years from official registration; the fetched page does not explicitly resolve eligibility for unregistered teams or multiple entries from one team. Confirm these before committing to a second application. Do not reuse the previous year's rules as current rules. [R12]

|---|---|---|
| 10–16 September | Interviews, scope, first reviewer, intake prototype | A specific recurring job and supported segment are agreed; sources inventoried. |
| 17–23 September | First reviewed task family, deterministic engine, profile model | Source/applicability tests pass; unknown cases do not receive definitive answers. |
| 24–30 September | Authenticated owner workflow and accountant grants | Task/source history and negative permission tests pass. |
| 1–7 October | Outbox reminders, PDF plan, limited private documents | Restart/retry, access, backup restore and file-safety checks pass. |

| 15–21 October | Correction cycle and source-change demo | Issues triaged; performance and user-comprehension measurements recorded. |
| 22–28 October | Freeze features; evidence and security review | No unresolved critical legal/date/access issues in supported scope. |
| 29 October–1 November | Final application materials and rehearsal | All claims reconciled to evidence; submission buffer preserved. |

Capacity assumption: two builders, with one focusing on product/backend/security and one on UX/localization/pilot operations, plus external qualified content review. AI assistance is a tool, not a replacement for accountable professional review or a named team member. Budget actual available hours across TRUST and SMEGUARD. If SMEGUARD is the smaller parallel effort, reduce to one reviewed workflow, one accountant pilot and the source-change demonstration; do not cut permission or truthfulness gates.

### Prioritized implementation backlog

**P0 — trust foundation.** Implement source registry/versioning, explicit coverage states, profile revisions, reviewed rule tests, organization scoping, advisor grants, clear UI status and reproducible local setup. Acceptance: unknown input stays unknown; a stale/unapproved rule cannot issue a statutory date; one tenant cannot see another's files or tasks.

**P1 — useful recurring workflow.** Persist task occurrences, assignments, proof metadata and history; add outbox reminders, bounded exports, account deletion/export and review feedback. Acceptance: re-evaluation does not duplicate obligations; revoked advisers cannot access queued exports; failed notification jobs are visible and recoverable.

**P2 — paid-pilot readiness.** Obtain approved source coverage, human-reviewed Khmer copy, support terms, payment reconciliation, restore evidence and a measured user trial. Acceptance: demo/test accounts remain distinguishable from paying customers; no seeded metrics appear as traction.

**P3 — only after validation.** Expand practice work queues, permitted document extraction, paid-plan limits and additional reviewed sectors. Add real integrations only after API access, contractual terms, ownership and privacy are established.

## 12. Competition proposal and stage story

### One-sentence proposal

**SMEGUARD.KH is a Khmer-first business operating assistant that turns reviewed Cambodian regulatory sources and confirmed business facts into clear tasks, reminders and document evidence, helping owners and accountants coordinate recurring obligations.**

### Value proposition

Owners get clarity about their next action. Accountants reduce repeated document chasing and maintain a shared client history. Government gains a complementary route to better-prepared applicants and more understandable official requirements, without giving the product authority to decide compliance or inspect private businesses.

Innovation is not “we added AI”. It is the auditable connection from a business fact to a reviewed rule version to a task occurrence to its evidence, with safe handling of change and uncertainty. Efficiency is measured time saved on a defined workflow. Marketing is a tested practice-led route to paid customers. Those are the proposed pitch dimensions, not a claim about unpublished 2026 scoring weights.

### Three-minute demo

**0:00–0:30:** Introduce a fictional owner with a small shop, employees and certificates scattered across messages. Clearly label the account synthetic. Complete or replay the eight-step intake.

**0:30–1:10:** Show only the next three useful actions. Open one source/applicability card: relevant facts, authority, rule version, review date and missing information. No general chat answer and no fake all-clear score.

### Submission evidence and ask

Prepare a project profile, a live/synthetic-demo distinction, a 90-second video, a short architecture view, supported-coverage statement, permission/restore evidence, interview findings, paid trial receipts where permission permits, and an honest pricing model. Obtain consent before naming customers or displaying business documents. Do not claim reduced fines, national coverage, regulator endorsement, verified integrations or revenue unless the underlying evidence exists.

The initial institutional ask should be introductions to suitable pilot businesses and qualified source reviewers, plus permission to reference publicly available sources accurately. It should not be an unsupported demand for national procurement or full government data access.
